About us

About GenBionex

GenBionex Consulting Company Ltd provides professional livestock breeding and management services built around modern genetic technology — helping Uganda's herds improve on evidence, not guesswork.

Why GenBionex

Breeding decisions should be provable.

Breeding decisions in Uganda are too often made without the data to back them — which animals to keep, which to cull, which pairings actually improve a herd. GenBionex exists to bring the same genetic rigour used by established breeding companies to Ugandan farms, at a scale that fits a working enterprise rather than a research programme.

How an engagement runs

Start to review, nothing templated.

Every engagement starts with a farm visit and an honest baseline of where your herd stands. From there we write a breeding goal specific to your enterprise, help put the protocols into practice, and return to measure progress against that goal. The plan is built for your scale, your market, and your animals.

Standards we work within

Operating within Uganda's livestock framework.

We operate within Uganda's livestock and veterinary regulatory framework — the environment shaped by bodies including MAAIF, NAGRC&DB, and the Uganda Veterinary Board — and handle herd and farm data with the same care we'd want for our own.
