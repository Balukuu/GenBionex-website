Working with us

What working with us includes.

Every engagement is built for your scale, your market, and your animals — nothing templated.

  • Plans built for your scale
  • Scheduled farm visits
  • Written protocols you keep
  • Herd data handled securely
  • Sourcing & equipment support
  • Plain-language reporting

How it works

Four steps, start to review.

  1. Farm visit & baseline

    We visit your farm and record where your herd stands today.

  2. Written breeding goal

    You receive a written breeding goal matched to your enterprise.

  3. Implementation

    We support you putting the plan and protocols into practice.

  4. Evaluation & review

    Progress is measured, reported plainly, and the goal is revisited.
