Our services

Nine services, five areas of a working herd.

We work across advisory and farm systems, breeding, genetics, laboratory and equipment, and nutrition. Every engagement starts with your herd and enterprise, not a fixed package.

Advisory & Farm Systems

Professional advice on farm animal management

Day-to-day management decisions backed by an outside, experienced eye.

  • Housing and handling system assessment
  • Practical recommendations you can implement

Farm planning — initial setup of a farm enterprise

A sound plan before you invest in stock or sheds.

  • Site layout and farm structure design
  • Realistic stocking rate and budget
  • Phased setup timeline and sequence

Farm enterprise evaluation

A real-time assessment of your enterprise's performance.

  • Production and cost performance review
  • Setting realistic industry targets
  • Clear priorities for improvement

Breeding

Setting and refining breeding goals

A written breeding goal matched to your enterprise and market.

  • Traits ranked by clear priority
  • Realistic targets with set timelines
  • Reviewed as your herd changes

Sourcing and selection of breeding animals

Breeding animals selected against your written goal, not guesswork.

  • Source identification and initial screening
  • Selection scored against breeding goal
  • Independent pre-purchase animal evaluation visit

Genetics

Genetic services

DNA-based services to support your breeding goals.

  • Animal ownership dispute resolution
  • On-farm DNA sample collection
  • Laboratory sample processing & advice on storage
  • Sequencing and genotyping
  • Breeding value estimation

Sample collection is on-farm. Laboratory processing partner: to be confirmed.

Nutrition

Feeds and feeding consultations

Feeding matched to production stage and what's locally available.

  • Ration review and redesign work
  • Local feed resource availability
  • Feeding plans by production stage

Laboratory & Equipment

Advice on setting up a breeding support laboratory

Guidance on the lab you need to support breeding decisions.

  • Equipment list and layout advice
  • Workflow and protocol design support
  • Staffing and training needs review

Supply and maintenance of biomedical equipment, and related consumables

Equipment and consumables sourced, installed, and kept working.

  • Equipment sourcing and reliable supply
  • Installation and scheduled maintenance visits
  • Ongoing consumables supply and restocking
